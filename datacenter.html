The Work

What You’d Be Doing

Data center builds are big, fast and built to a standard. The electrical scope is real work for people who know the trade.

Power Distribution

Switchgear, panels, transformers, feeders and terminations through the distribution rooms that keep a building live.

Conduit & Cable Tray

Bending, racking and pulling at scale, to print and to spec, across white space and support areas.

Critical Systems

UPS and generator rooms, standby power, grounding and bonding — the systems a data center cannot be handed over without.
